# Merchant UPI Soundbox Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 18, 2026

Merchant UPI Soundbox reads supported payment notifications on your Android device so it can announce confirmed UPI payments. Notification processing, duplicate detection, announcement preferences, and the audit history stay on your device. The app does not upload notification contents, payment amounts, UPI identifiers, contacts, or personal information to the developer.

The app uses Android notification access and text-to-speech features only for its core payment-announcement function. You can revoke notification access at any time in Android Settings and clear app data by uninstalling the app.

The developer does not sell or share user data. The app is not directed to children. Future versions that change these practices will update this policy and the Google Play Data safety disclosure before release.
